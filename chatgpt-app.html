The Astro Yuvam app lets you use Astro Yuvam's astrology and numerology calculation engine inside ChatGPT. It has five read-only tools: Birth Chart, Current Sky, Life Path and Name Numbers, Daylight Saving Check and Ask the Sky (traditional horary astrology). Positions are calculated with Swiss Ephemeris and historical time-zone rules. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no payments and no advertising.

1. Privacy Policy

What data the app receives

ChatGPT sends the app only the fields needed for the calculation you ask for:

  • Birth Chart: birth date, birth time (optional), birth city and, if needed, country, house system, and an optional name shown on the chart wheel.
  • Current Sky: no personal data (only the language of your message).
  • Life Path and Name Numbers: birth date and, optionally, a name to analyse.
  • Daylight Saving Check: a date, a clock time and optionally a city.
  • Ask the Sky: the text of your question, its type and topic, and the city you are in if you mention it. If you do not, ChatGPT may pass a coarse location (city/country level); it is used only to cast the chart for the place of the question. The app never asks for precise location (GPS or address).

The app does not request or receive e-mail addresses, phone numbers, account identifiers, payment details, government IDs or health records. ChatGPT may attach technical metadata to each request (such as locale or an anonymous identifier); the app does not use or store it.

Purpose

Data is used only to perform the requested calculation and return the result (chart wheel, positions, numbers or the horary verdict) to ChatGPT.

Retention

Nothing you send is stored. Inputs, including question texts, are not written to a database or log files and are discarded when the calculation ends. To prevent abuse we keep daily request counters that are not linked to a person. City names and coordinates may be cached temporarily, without any link to a user, to speed up look-ups.

Recipients

  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API: only the city (and country) name is sent, to find its coordinates and time zone.
  • Hosting: the calculation server runs on our hosting provider (Render), which may be located outside Türkiye.
  • No data from the app is sent to any AI service, advertiser or data broker, and no data is sold.

Your choices

You can disconnect or remove the app at any time in ChatGPT settings. Because we keep no records, there is nothing to delete; you can still contact us with any request at astroyuvam@gmail.com. Your conversation in ChatGPT is processed under OpenAI's own privacy policy.

Age

The app is not directed at children under 13.

The full privacy policy of the Astro Yuvam website (in Turkish, under Turkish data-protection law KVKK) is available at astroyuvam.com/gizlilik-kvkk.html.
