How We Calculate

Every result starts from the actual sky at your birth, not from pre-written sun-sign text.

☉︎

Real planetary positions

The planets, your Ascendant and Midheaven, the houses, the aspects and the North Node are calculated with a high-precision ephemeris, and the results are checked against the Swiss Ephemeris.

♈︎

Tropical zodiac, Placidus houses

We use the tropical zodiac of Western astrology. Houses are Placidus by default, and the birth chart calculator lets you switch to Koch, Equal House or Whole Sign.

◷

Historical time zones and DST

Your local birth time is converted to Universal Time using the time-zone and daylight saving rules that applied in your birthplace on that date. An hour's error here can change your rising sign.

✦

Calculated fresh for you

Nothing comes from a lookup table. Your chart is calculated for your exact date, time and place each time you ask for it.

Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a science. We take care to get the astronomy right; what it means is yours to reflect on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you start.

Are the tools really free?
Yes. All five tools are free to use, with no sign-up and no email address required. Your result appears right on the page.
Do I need my exact birth time?
It helps a lot. Your rising sign (Ascendant), Midheaven and houses depend on the exact time. If you leave the time out, the birth chart calculator draws your chart for 12:00 noon and leaves those parts out. Planet signs stay largely accurate, but the Moon moves about 13° a day, so its sign can be uncertain if you were born on a day it changed signs.
Which zodiac and house system do you use?
The tropical zodiac, which is the standard in Western astrology. Houses are calculated with Placidus by default, and the birth chart calculator also lets you switch to Koch, Equal House or Whole Sign.
Why might my chart look different on another site?
Usually it comes down to the birth time or the time zone. If daylight saving time is applied incorrectly, the whole chart shifts by an hour, which can change your rising sign and houses. A different house system can also move planets into different houses. The signs of the planets themselves should match any calculator that uses the tropical zodiac.
Is astrology scientific?
No. Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a science, and there is no scientific evidence that the planets shape personality or events. The astronomy behind your chart is precise; the meanings are for self-reflection and fun. Please don't use them in place of medical, legal or financial advice.
